# GSAK authors' permissions

Some GSAK macro authors have given general permission for their macros to be
ported to OpenSAK and published here under the MIT License. If an author is
listed below, you don't need to ask them again before porting one of their
macros — just give the credit they ask for.

For everyone else, see [Porting a GSAK macro](../CONTRIBUTING.md#porting-a-gsak-macro).

| GSAK author | Permission | Credit asked for | Given |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kai Team (Bob Long) | All of Kai Team's GSAK macros may be ported to OpenSAK and covered by MIT | Credit to "Kai Team" in the macro's README | 18 November 2026, in the OpenSAK Facebook group and by e-mail to Allan (AgreeDK) |
| Gill & Tony (Tony Rolfe) | Any of Tony Rolfe's GSAK macros and ideas may be used in OpenSAK macros | A comment that the code is based on ideas by Tony Rolfe (Gill & Tony) | 18 November 2026, by e-mail to Ian Cole (ianwok), quoted in [#25](https://github.com/OpenSAK-Org/OpenSAK-Macros/pull/25) |

## Adding an author

If a GSAK author gives general permission, a maintainer adds a row here in a
small pull request, with where and when the permission was given.
